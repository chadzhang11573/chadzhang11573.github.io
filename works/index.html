three three two
c. 2025
instrumentation: clarinet in Bb, horn in F, bassoon, viola, violoncello, percussion (marimba, snare drum, suspended cymbal [with bow])

for score inquiries please contact adrian.b.zhang@gmail.com

three three two is a play on the phrase itself; the first and second movements are in ternary form, while the third and final movement is in binary form. throughout the piece, and especially in the first movement, san/san/er, the three-three-two ostinato is a significant motif. 3'32", the pointillistic second movement, aims to last that exact duration. lastly, TWO!!! ...and one is a celebratory final movement that uses the ideas of movements one and two.

(re)birth
c. 2024
for large orchestra

for score inquiries please contact adrian.b.zhang@gmail.com

(re)birth explores the cycle of birth, life, death, and rebirth. it signifies this cycle through the deliberate placement of ideas from the harmonic series; these ideas both begin and end certain passages, as well as the piece itself. as (re)birth weaves between mechanical and organic structures, it demonstrates that the events between periods of growth and subsequent decline are complex. similarly, the piece shifts suddenly from open and lyrical to ambient and discreet, ultimately building to a powerful climax. after all, to be reborn is to change significantly.

memories
c. 2024
for harp, piano, vibraphone, flute, and cello

for score inquiries please contact adrian.b.zhang@gmail.com

what are memories? whether vivid or opaque, gripping or distant, our memories emerge as fragmented constellations of our lived experiences. when we encounter a sight or sound that feels vaguely familiar, we are likely sensing the resonating echo from an obscure memory just beyond our conscious reach. a majority of "memories" consists of sound fragments — short or long, played once or repeated — a fluctuating structure that reflects the indeterminate nature of memories. consequently, "memories" is notated proportionally, not conforming to a certain length or duration. some parts are left to the performer's interpretation, with each musician potentially perceiving the same material differently.

meltdown I
c. 2024
instrumentation: flute, bass clarinet in Bb, large gong (doubling bass drum), vibraphone, piano, violin, viola, cello

for score inquiries please contact adrian.b.zhang@gmail.com

the word "meltdown" has multiple unique meanings in different contexts. this piece explores one such meaning — that of an environmental meltdown. images of an arctic scene slowly, then explosively progress into crashing icebergs and the cutting of trees as the piece progresses. mixed feelings ranging from ambient neutrality to spiraling confusion are all evoked, symbolizing humanity's dangerously comforting dilemma.

war
c. 2023
instrumentation: piano, violin, viola, cello

for score inquiries please contact adrian.b.zhang@gmail.com

mvt. I: conflict symbolizes the violence of warfare. material repeats at certain intervals like attacks in war. such materials use "inverse chords": chords with the same complexity, but opposite feelings. mvt. II: desolation starts with slow chords interrupted by unrelated fragments. mvt. III: death is announced by the tritone of the piano ringing forth like the bell of death.
